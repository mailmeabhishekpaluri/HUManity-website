A CHILD IS MORE THAN THEIR CIRCUMSTANCES

Not just a place to grow.
A chance to thrive.

Children in institutional care deserve the same opportunities to learn, belong and imagine a future as any other child.

HUManity brings volunteers, partners and childcare institutions together around the whole child — their learning, emotional well-being, health and hopes for tomorrow.
