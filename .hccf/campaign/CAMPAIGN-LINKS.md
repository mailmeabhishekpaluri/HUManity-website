For each Meta ad, use its matching home page as the destination. This takes the visitor directly to that home's story and donation form.

Example with campaign labels:

[Macharam Instagram campaign](https://humanityorg.foundation/wheelsofhope/fund/gypsy-home-macharam/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=hccf_macharam&utm_content=story_01)

Give each home's campaign a distinct `utm_campaign`. Optional `ad_id`, `adset_id` and `campaign_id` labels are saved in the donation dashboard. Keep personal information out of URL parameters.

The pages are prepared for separate campaigns. Before spending on ads, complete Razorpay webhook configuration and connect/test the Meta conversion dataset. URL labels alone do not provide Meta donation conversion tracking.
