HUManity · Mission 2030

Every CCI visible,
connected and supported.

Our ambition is to bring 220 childcare institutions onto a shared platform by 2030, learning from a 15-CCI pilot.

What we’re doing now

01

Listening to homes

Research across 45 CCIs has informed the selection of 10 institutions. CCI-led discussions will help shape the pilot.

02

Making needs visible

Documenting institutional needs and building toward district-wise profiles that help communities understand where support is needed.

03

Bringing support closer

Connecting home-specific fundraising and community contributions with HUManity’s ongoing work in education, emotional well-being, digital literacy, health and libraries.

15 CCIs is the pilot target; 220 is the ambition for 2030. These are future goals, not institutions already reached through the platform.
