What does ₹2,000 a month support?

₹2,000 a month supports one child through HUManity’s childcare work. Your contribution helps meet everyday care and learning needs. Gifts are used across this work according to need; this page does not assign an individual child to a donor.

Why do you need my name and government ID?

We ask for your name as per government ID, contact details and ID information to maintain donation records and provide your donation receipt and 80G documentation. Eligible donations to HUManity qualify for a 50% deduction under Section 80G, subject to applicable tax rules and limits. Your details are kept private.

How does the 50% benefit under Section 80G work?

Eligible donors can deduct 50% of the qualifying donation from taxable income, subject to applicable qualifying limits. It is a deduction from taxable income, not a refund of half your donation. Section 80G is not available to individuals choosing the new tax regime. HUManity provides the relevant donation documentation.

How do monthly donations work?

Your selected amount is charged monthly through a Razorpay subscription. The subscription continues until cancelled, for up to 120 monthly instalments. The yearly amount shown is an illustration of 12 months of giving, not an upfront charge. Contact contact@humanityorg.foundation or +91 99129 62885 for cancellation assistance.

What if my payment is debited but not confirmed?

Use Check payment confirmation on this page before making another payment. If confirmation is still pending, contact HUManity at contact@humanityorg.foundation or +91 99129 62885 with your Razorpay payment reference.
