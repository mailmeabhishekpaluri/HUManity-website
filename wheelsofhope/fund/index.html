GIVE WITH CLARITY

A few things
you may want to know.

How were these homes selected?

These homes were selected from the HCCF field assessment and the updated support list. Their requirements differ: some need repairs and basic essentials, while Meyers Balabhavan has a specific digital-learning need.

Do all the homes need the same kind of support?

Each home has its own priorities. Some need safe roofs, beds or dependable essentials. Others need learning equipment or support for a new facility. Read the individual story to see the needs identified during the field assessment.

What does the ₹6 lakh goal cover?

It is the annual campaign target for each home. The home-specific story lists identified needs. Item-wise budgets and allocations will be confirmed before procurement; technical and construction work requires further checks.

How does monthly giving count toward the goal?

A confirmed ₹500 monthly donation represents a ₹6,000 commitment over 12 months. ₹1,000 monthly represents ₹12,000. We show this annual commitment toward the goal, alongside the amount actually received. Future instalments are not money already collected.

What happens if I need to stop monthly giving?

Contact HUManity at contact@humanityorg.foundation or +91 99129 62885 for help with your subscription. When cancellation or an inactive subscription is confirmed, future instalments are removed from the committed total.
