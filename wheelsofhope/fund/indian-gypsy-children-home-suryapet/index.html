THE HOME BEHIND YOUR GIFT

The Indian Gypsy Children Home

Bhukya Dhanpal and his wife have maintained the Indian Gypsy Children Home since 2002. Today, fifteen boys live here. The home receives limited outside support and depends largely on local people and individual contributions.

The field assessment recorded small rooms in the older building and gaps in water, bedding, laundry, storage and educational equipment. The supplied photographs show the spaces the children use each day.

A new building is under construction but remains incomplete. This fundraiser prioritises practical, documented needs that can improve daily life, while any construction support will require further verification.

Before you give

Will my donation be recorded for this home?

Yes. Donations made on this page are recorded against The Indian Gypsy Children Home in Suryapet. Its progress is separate from other HCCF homes.

How does monthly giving count toward the goal?

A confirmed ₹500 monthly donation represents a ₹6,000 commitment over 12 months. ₹1,000 monthly represents ₹12,000. We show this annual commitment toward the goal, alongside the amount actually received. Future instalments are not money already collected.

What happens if I need to stop monthly giving?

Contact HUManity at contact@humanityorg.foundation or +91 99129 62885 for help with your subscription. When cancellation or an inactive subscription is confirmed, future instalments are removed from the committed total.

Why do you ask for my name and ID details?

The donation form collects your name as shown on your government ID, contact information, and ID type and number for donation records and acknowledgement. These details are not shown in public fundraising totals.

Who can I contact about a payment?

Contact contact@humanityorg.foundation or +91 99129 62885 with your Razorpay payment reference. If your bank shows a debit but confirmation is pending, use Check payment confirmation rather than making another payment.
