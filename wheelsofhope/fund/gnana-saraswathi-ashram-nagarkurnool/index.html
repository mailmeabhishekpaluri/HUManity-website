THE HOME BEHIND YOUR GIFT

Gnana Saraswathi Ashram

Gnana Saraswathi Ashram in Nagarkurnool began in 2017 and is associated with Bhupal Reddy. The temple and daily Annadanam provide core support for the home, which currently accommodates ten boys.

During the visit, the team recorded wall cracks and maintenance concerns, around two washrooms, and older iron beds that need replacement. These are needs that deserve careful assessment and practical follow-through.

Computers and stronger medical linkages would also support the boys' development. Partial construction exists at the site; any construction support will depend on checking the documents, technical requirements and funding position.

Before you give

Will my donation be recorded for this home?

Yes. Donations made on this page are recorded against Gnana Saraswathi Ashram in Nagarkurnool. Its progress is separate from other HCCF homes.

How does monthly giving count toward the goal?

A confirmed ₹500 monthly donation represents a ₹6,000 commitment over 12 months. ₹1,000 monthly represents ₹12,000. We show this annual commitment toward the goal, alongside the amount actually received. Future instalments are not money already collected.

What happens if I need to stop monthly giving?

Contact HUManity at contact@humanityorg.foundation or +91 99129 62885 for help with your subscription. When cancellation or an inactive subscription is confirmed, future instalments are removed from the committed total.

Why do you ask for my name and ID details?

We need your name as per government ID, contact details and ID information to maintain donation records and provide your donation receipt and 80G documentation. Eligible donations to HUManity qualify for a 50% deduction under Section 80G, subject to applicable tax rules and limits. Your details are kept private and are not displayed on the fundraiser.

What does the 50% 80G benefit mean?

Eligible donors can deduct 50% of the qualifying donation from taxable income, subject to the applicable qualifying limit. This is a deduction from taxable income, not a refund of half your donation. Section 80G is not available to individuals choosing the new tax regime. HUManity provides the relevant donation documentation.

Who can I contact about a payment?

Contact contact@humanityorg.foundation or +91 99129 62885 with your Razorpay payment reference. If your bank shows a debit but confirmation is pending, use Check payment confirmation rather than making another payment.
